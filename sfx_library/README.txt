AutoEditor built-in sound effects (27 categories, 3 variations each).
All sounds were synthesized from scratch by tools/make_sfx.py - they are original
and free to use in your videos (no licence needed).

Transitions: whoosh, swoosh, swipe, reverse_whoosh, riser
Hits:        impact, boom, bass_drop, hit_cinematic, punch
Typing / UI: typing, typewriter, key_click, mouse_click, camera, pop, ding, error, glitch
Mood:        tension, rumble, heartbeat, clock, sparkle
Other:       cash, page_turn, engine_rev

To use your own sounds: make an SFX folder with the same sub-folder names (or new
ones) and choose it as "SFX library" in the app - your files take priority, and
categories you don't have fall back to these built-in ones.
Keywords that trigger each sound are in sfx_rules.json.
Only add sounds you have the rights to (your own, royalty-free, or licensed).
